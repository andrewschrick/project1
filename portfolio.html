Portfolio

Sim Racing Steering Wheel

In this project, I completely designed and built a custom sim racing steering wheel. I designed the wheel in CAD, 3D printed the body, and assembled the PCB. The wheel features a custom PCB with an ESP32 microcontroller using shift register logic to expand the I/O. The inputs include rotary encoders, push buttons, hall effect clutches, and a 7 way funcky switch. The wheel is running a custom firmware, recognized by Windows as a standard input device.

Vehicle Status Warning System

As a member of Texas A&M University's Formula Electric team, I designed and built a visual and audible warning system for our racecar. The light system bightly displays the status of the car's battery system, while the audible buzzer alerts anyone in the vicinity when the vehicle is precharged and ready to drive. The system is powered by a custom PCB with an STM32 microcontroller, and is running a custom firmware.

Wakeboard Training Winch

In this project, I designed and built a remote controlled winch for solo wakeboard training, eliminating the need for a boat and driver. The winch is powered by a small bore Honda engine and is controlled by an RC remote. This project was a collaboration with a friend, where I designed and built the electronics and control system, in addition to the fabrication of the winch. The winch features a custom PCB with an ESP32 microcontroller, and is running a custom firmware. The winch is capable of pulling a wakeboarder at speeds up to 30 mph from 600 feet away.
